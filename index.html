About MUSPIRATION

Who We Are

MUSPIRATION is a music-first creative media brand. We explore music, spotlight the people behind it and tell the stories that give creative work its meaning. Music is where we begin. The wider creative world, from film and dance to theatre and writing, is where the conversation can go.

  • Music.Knowledge, craft and the sound itself.
  • People.The creatives and educators behind the work.
  • Stories.The journeys and conversations that give it meaning.

Our Purpose

To give creativity a voice, visibility and a platform by celebrating expression, discovering inspiring people, sharing meaningful stories and encouraging conversations.

Our Mission

To create engaging, informative, entertaining and thought-provoking media that connects audiences with music, creative talent, industry stories and individuals shaping creative spaces.

Our Vision

To become a recognised and influential creative media platform that connects audiences with creatives, tells impactful stories, hosts meaningful conversations and documents the growth of the creative industry.

What We Explore

Six areas. One creative world.

Creativity is worth exploring, and the people behind it deserve to be heard. These are the areas MUSPIRATION is built around.

  • 01

    Music and Music Education

    Music knowledge, theory and musicianship. Instruments, learning and the developments shaping the music industry.

  • 02

    People and Interviews

    Conversations, interviews and profiles with musicians, artists, educators and other creative individuals.

  • 03

    Stories and Creative Experiences

    Personal journeys, behind-the-scenes experiences and the creative processes behind the work.

  • 04

    Entertainment and Industry Trends

    Developments, news and conversations about the trends shaping music and entertainment.

  • 05

    Opinions, Debates and Discussions

    Thought-provoking questions and different perspectives on music and creativity.

  • 06

    Film and Wider Creative Arts

    Stories from filmmaking, dance, acting, theatre, writing and other connected creative disciplines.

Music is the centre. Everything else is welcome where it connects to music, people or stories.

Meet the Founder

Emmanuel Chinecherem Chidiadi

Also known as D-stringspirationist

  • Music enthusiast
  • Pianist
  • Violinist
  • Music educator in the making

Emmanuel Chinecherem Chidiadi, also known as D-stringspirationist, is a music enthusiast, pianist, violinist and Music student at the University of Port Harcourt (UNIPORT), Nigeria. He is also a music educator in the making. With a background in music learning and teaching, and a growing academic interest in music, he founded MUSPIRATION to make music easier to understand, give creative people a voice and tell the stories behind the sound.

Why he founded MUSPIRATION

  1. To make music easier to understand.
  2. To give creative people a voice.
  3. To tell the stories behind the sound.
